Tier 5 of five, read straight out of art/knight-camp.png — that file is the warrior's ladder, and the row is one number to change when the others are wanted. He does not sit in the middle of his cell: the greatsword hangs to one side, so the centre of the artwork and the centre of the man are different, and the anchor above is the man. The shadow is his own outline projected onto the plane — the sprite's own alpha, leaned away from the sun by height / tan(elevation) and softened with distance from his boots — so it moves when he does and cannot fall out of agreement with the pose. It belongs to this page: the bake carries none and the game draws the hero none.

24 is a duration, not a frame rate. Mixamo authored this idle at 30 fps and it runs 2.000 seconds — 61 keys. The sheet holds 2.000 × 12 = 24 cells and plays them at 12, so it lasts exactly as long as the clip does: nothing is sped up and nothing is cut. What 12 costs is three keys in every five, which an idle can spare and a swing could not. Thirty would want 60 cells, and 60 × 350 is 21,000 pixels in one row — past the 16,384 a browser will hold as a texture, which is the actual reason the rate is 12 and not the other way about.
