    Cells are 350 × 540 throughout, and the sole line is fixed at 465 for every stance. What moves inside the cell is the man: the lowest pixel rises 70 on the casting and 38 on the inward slash, because he lunges and leaves the ground. The anchor is the cell centre — the bake's camera axis — since the footprint centre swings from 160 on the run to 198 on the casting, and pinning to any one of those slides all the others sideways. The shadow is his own outline projected onto the plane, leaned away from the sun by height / tan(elevation), so it moves when he does. It belongs to this page: the bake carries none and the game draws the hero none.

    24 is a duration, not a frame rate. Mixamo authored this idle at 30 fps and it runs 2.000 seconds — 61 keys. The sheet holds 2.000 × 12 = 24 cells and plays them at 12, so it lasts exactly as long as the clip does: nothing is sped up and nothing is cut. What 12 costs is three keys in every five, which an idle can spare and a swing could not. Thirty would want 60 cells, and 60 × 350 is 21,000 pixels in one row — past the 16,384 a browser will hold as a texture, which is the actual reason the rate is 12 and not the other way about.
