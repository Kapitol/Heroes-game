Crypt Heroes · build ontology

Every major part of the game, and how finished it actually is.

An isometric idle auto-battler: the hero walks a road, fights without being told, and you spend the cooldowns and the skulls. This maps the parts that make that happen — five strata, from the loop underneath to the assets on top — and marks each one against evidence in the repo rather than intention.

How these were judged

Forged means seen working, not merely written — most of stratum IV was screenshotted in a running fight. Rough is almost always coverage rather than quality: the system works and the content behind it is uneven, which is why the biomes, the foes and the camps all land there together.

Two parts are cast aside and that is not a defect. The jointed rig and the painted class sheets both work; they were beaten by something that reads better and cannot do what they do. The armoury is the real cost of that trade, and it is the largest open question in the game — larger than any single row above.
